All In One POS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

All In One POS (“the App”) is an offline-first point-of-sale application for Myanmar retailers, with an optional online account, cloud sync, a web storefront and a renewal website (shop.allinonepos.app). This policy describes what data they process and why.

Data we process

  • Shop business data you enter: products, stock, sales, orders, customers, suppliers, expenses, cash register sessions and related records. Stored on your device and, when cloud sync is on (Premium), on our hosted backend (Supabase), separated by shop.
  • Account data if you create an online account: your email address and sign-in credentials. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account's email address and an account identifier from Google. Staff accounts you invite are identified by the email address you enter.
  • Device data: a device identifier and when each device was last active, used to keep a shop to its device limit (up to 3 on Premium) and to verify Premium.
  • Billing data if you buy or renew Premium: your plan, expiry, and payment records (amount, date, payment method and reference). Card payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy and MMQR payments by MyanMyanPay; we never receive your full card number. If you pay by bank transfer, the screenshot you upload is kept as a billing record.
  • Web storefront orders: when a customer orders from a shop's web storefront, we store the name, phone number and delivery address they enter, an optional payment screenshot, and the IP address the order came from, so the shop can fulfil the order and block abusive orders. The shop owns these records; we process them on the shop's behalf.
  • Photos: product photos and shop logos you upload to the cloud. Expense receipt photos stay on your device and are not uploaded.
  • Email: account messages (confirmation, password reset) and reminders before and after Premium expires, sent to your account email.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports (via Sentry) with device model, OS, app version and the error — not your sales ledger.
  • Support: anything you choose to send us, such as Viber messages or your App Reference ID.

How we use data

  • To run the POS, inventory, orders, invoices, sync, storefront and Premium features.
  • To verify Premium, apply device limits and prevent misuse, including repeat free trials (one per owner account).
  • To send account emails and Premium expiry reminders.
  • To fix crashes and improve reliability.

We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it.

Who we share it with

Only service providers that run parts of the service for us, each receiving only what that job needs:

  • Supabase — database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Vercel — hosting for our websites (storefront, renewal page, admin).
  • Google — only if you choose Sign in with Google.
  • Lemon Squeezy and MyanMyanPay — payment processing for Premium.
  • Resend — sending email.
  • Sentry — crash reporting.

A shop's public storefront shows only the catalogue and shop details the shop chooses to publish.

Retention

Data on your device stays until you clear it or uninstall the App. Cloud data stays while your account exists. Crash reports are kept by Sentry for a limited period. When you delete your account, see below for what is removed and what is kept.

Delete your account and data

In the App (shop owner): open Shop → Account → Delete account and confirm with your password, or with Google if you signed in with Google.

Without the App: message Support on Viber at 09740022900 from the account owner, with your account email address and shop name. We verify that you own the account and delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted: your online account and the staff logins of shops you own; those shops' cloud data — products, stock, sales, orders, customers, suppliers, expenses, cash sessions, devices, storefront and storefront orders; and the shops' uploaded files, including product photos, logos and customers' payment screenshots.

What is kept: Premium subscription and payment records, and payment screenshots you sent for Premium, for as long as needed for accounting, tax and fraud prevention. Payment providers keep their own records under their own policies.

Trial-claim records are deleted with the account; the once-per-owner-account trial limit applies while that account exists.

Deleting your account does not erase data stored on your device: the App returns to the Free plan and your local records stay until you clear them or uninstall the App.

Your choices

  • You can use the Free plan without creating an account; your data then stays on your device.
  • Camera, Bluetooth, photos and notifications are requested only when you use the related feature.

Children

The App is for business use by adults and is not intended for children under 13.

Contact

Use Support in the App, or Viber 09740022900.
